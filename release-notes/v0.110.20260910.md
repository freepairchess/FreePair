# FreePair v0.110.20260910

## What's new

### Clearer pairing explanations

- **Know which opponent a result refers to.** The Standings number column
  is labelled **Standings #**. Hover a round cell to see the opponent's
  name, pair number and standings number. Wall Chart tooltips use pair numbers.
- **No missing opponents in comparison diagrams.** The USCF score-group
  comparison uses recorded pairings to identify outgoing players instead
  of assuming the lowest-rated player floated. Where a complete comparison
  cannot be drawn, it explains why and lists the recorded pairs and histories.
- **Reconstructed comparisons are labelled honestly.** Purple lines show
  differences from a reconstructed rating split, not proof of why the engine
  chose a pairing. Recognized engine diagnostic references use roster pair
  numbers; unfamiliar wording is explicitly labelled as engine-rank text.
  These changes do not alter the pairing algorithm, scores or colours.

### Roster cleanup after play starts

- **Remove players who only received byes.** Eligible players can be deleted
  after any round, then permanently removed from the roster, saved event and
  future USCF reports. Players referenced in pending, played or forfeited
  opponent games remain protected; other players' numbers are preserved.
- **Permanent deletion is visibly distinct.** A soft-deleted player's trash
  icon turns red. Restoring the player turns it green again. Permanent deletion
  still requires confirmation.

### Settings and display improvements

- **Preview themes before keeping them.** Theme names are alphabetical.
  Select a theme to preview it, choose **Keep theme** to save, or **Revert**
  to restore the last kept choice. Closing Settings discards an unkept preview;
  other preferences still save immediately.
- **One global grid-text control.** Text size and font controls sit at the
  far right of the section tab-name row rather than repeating on each table.
  Tabs scroll without overlapping the controls, and table content stays full-width.
- **No repeated automatic rating-refresh prompts after round one.** Later
  pairing actions keep the roster's ratings. Explicit manual verification or
  refresh still warns before changing a roster with existing pairings.
- **Publisher information in About.** About identifies Chessnique LLC and
  FreePair's copyright while retaining existing attribution and disclaimers.

## Installer

- **Windows x64 only for this release.** Run `FreePair-win-x64-Setup.exe`.
  The installer bundles the .NET runtime, pairing engines and updated guide.
  Keep your normal event backups before updating.
- **Signed as Chessnique LLC.** Windows executables are signed and timestamped
  through the build pipeline, and Setup uses the FreePair logo.
- **Other platforms remain on v0.109.20260910.** No new macOS or Linux packages
  are included in this Windows update.

## Known limitations

- **Existing pairing differences remain.** A clearer explanation does not
  establish that every pairing is optimal or matches another program.
- **Old diagnostic prose is not retroactively regenerated.** Newly generated
  USCF annotations receive the player-number translation; earlier saved notes
  may retain their original numbering.
- **Deletion does not rewrite previously exported reports.** Export a new report
  after changing the roster. Permanent deletion cannot be undone with Restore.
- **Security prompts can still appear.** Signing does not override SmartScreen,
  antivirus policy or administrator permissions.
