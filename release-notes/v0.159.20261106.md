# FreePair v0.159.20261106

## What's new

### NA Chess Hub membership and sign-in

- **Pair offline for up to 14 days with a verified membership.** Once signed in and verified online, FreePair securely saves the signed membership proof for local pairing across restarts and normal app upgrades. Access ends at the earlier of the proof's signed expiry and membership expiry; FreePair renews the proof while online. Hub roster sync, event downloads and publishing still require internet.
- **See exactly when offline access ends.** Account status shows the verified proof's expiry in UTC and your computer's local time and UTC offset. Clock-related failures show both the signed time and the computer's time, with platform-specific clock-sync guidance. If the proof cannot be saved or its signing key is not trusted offline, Account status warns you.
- **Linux and macOS receive the recent Windows sign-in and saving improvements.** Sign-in asks you to choose the correct account; brief event-file access conflicts are retried, and Sync Changes lists recent Hub edits first.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and pairing engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and chess pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **Offline access ends at the signed expiry.** You must first verify membership online; it cannot be extended without reconnecting. Signing out clears the saved proof. Stay on the same computer account and keep its secure credential store available; a signing-key change may require a FreePair update before a new proof can be stored for offline use.
- **Online event features still need a connection.** Hub roster sync, event downloads and publishing do not work offline, even when local pairing is unlocked.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
