# FreePair v0.155.20261021

## What's new

- **Continue your NA Chess Hub event from a local file.** Organizers and delegated tournament directors can reopen a saved SJSON event and continue pairing without membership. Sign in to the account that manages the event; FreePair checks access with NA Chess Hub online. Your saved pairings and results remain in the local working file.
- **See your account status at a glance.** Login, membership reported by NA Chess Hub, and verified FreePair access now appear as separate, clearly labeled sections with indented status values. The Free use & membership button explains the available options.
- **Know what to do when access is blocked.** An access-limited action now opens a dialog explaining the free local limit (20 players, four rounds), free access to your authorized NA Chess Hub events, and which other events need a verified membership. The dialog includes a membership purchase button and a selectable link. Seeing an active membership in your account does not replace the signed access check.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon, and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and pairing engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **Local file association is not tamper-proof.** FreePair checks that your signed-in account can manage the NA Chess Hub event ID in a local file, but cannot prove that manually changed file contents originally belonged to that event. Keep the original event file and use an account you trust.
- **Online verification is needed.** Reopening an event without a verified membership beyond the free local limits requires NA Chess Hub to be reachable. If verification fails, the local file stays intact and the limit remains in place.
- **Signed membership needs periodic renewal.** If NA Chess Hub's signing service is temporarily unavailable, previously verified membership access lasts only until its signed expiration. Small local events remain free.
- **Linux account sign-in needs a credential keyring.** Install and unlock a Secret Service-compatible keyring to store NA Chess Hub credentials securely.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
