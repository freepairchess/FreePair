# FreePair v0.59

A small **usability release**. Bye requests now have their own tab in the player
dialog instead of hiding below the identity fields, and the read-only "Bughouse
Pairing Section" marker no longer appears on ordinary sections.

## What's new in v0.59

### Bye requests are easier to find

- **Byes now have their own tab.** Open a player from the Roster and you'll see a
  **Bye Requests** tab alongside Required and Optional Info. Requested byes for
  upcoming rounds, byes for rounds a late entry missed, and corrections to byes
  in already-paired rounds all live there.
- **No more scrolling to the bottom.** These sections used to sit underneath the
  name, rating and section fields, so the one thing you often opened the dialog
  for was the hardest thing to reach.
- **The tab only appears when it applies.** Adding a player to a section that
  hasn't been paired yet has no byes to set, so the tab stays out of your way.

### Fewer confusing section settings

- **"Bughouse Pairing Section" is shown only on sections that are one.** A
  section becomes a Bughouse Pairing section when it's created that way — from NA
  Chess Hub or from a bughouse roster — and it can't be undone, because the rows
  in it are two-player teams. Ordinary sections no longer display an unchecked
  box that invites you into a setting you can't reverse.

  To update now, click "Check for updates" in the Settings tab.

The installer bundles the .NET 10 runtime, the pairing engines, and the
rules-reference document — no additional downloads or configuration required.

## Known limitations

- **Existing Bughouse Pairing sections are unaffected.** They still show the
  marker, and it remains read-only — this release only stops showing it where it
  doesn't apply.
- **Nothing changed about how byes work.** The choices, the rules about setting
  byes before a round is paired, and the effect on scores and standings are all
  the same; only where they appear in the dialog has moved.
