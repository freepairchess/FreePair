# FreePair v0.93.20260831

Windows release. macOS and Linux remain on `v0.90.20260829`.

## What's new

### The Live Score Board no longer shows pairings you have not accepted

- **A round you are still looking at in the preview stays off the
  board.** Pairing a round opens the preview so you can swap a colour,
  move a board or grant a late half-point bye before committing. Until
  now those unconfirmed pairings were already on the projector, on the
  board shared to phones and televisions, and on NA Chess Hub — for the
  whole time the preview was open, which is exactly when you are still
  deciding. They now appear the moment you accept, and not before.

- **A preview you cancel never reaches the room at all.** Previously the
  round appeared on the screen and then vanished when you cancelled,
  which could send players to boards that never existed.

- **It also stopped an unaccepted round reaching your event file.**
  FreePair auto-saves in the background, so a preview left open long
  enough could write a round you had not agreed to. Cancelling undid it,
  but a crash or a flat battery at the wrong moment would not have.

This applies to every round, not just the first.

### Live Score Board

- **The clock shows seconds.** On a screen a room reads to decide
  whether the round has started, a minute-only clock looks the same
  whether the board is live or frozen. A ticking second says it is
  working before anybody checks a board number against the sheet.

- **"Last updated at …" sits under the clock**, showing when a pairing or
  a result last changed — not when the board last refreshed itself.
  18:40 above *Last updated at 17:32* tells you an hour of scoresheets
  has not reached the desk.

- **You can close the board from the desk.** **Event Operations → Close
  Live Score Board** appears while one is running, and the setup panel
  grows a **Close Live Score Board** button. The board opens on the
  display your working window is *not* on, so until now the only way to
  stop it was to walk to that screen and press Escape.

- **Closing FreePair closes the board.** It used to be left running on a
  display you could no longer see, still showing the last round, after
  you had closed FreePair and packed up.

Sharing to phones and televisions is separate and keeps running when you
close the projector — turning a screen off is not the same as asking the
room to stop following the event.

### Roster

- **One copy button per player instead of six.** The copy icon used to
  sit inside the ID, Name, Rating, Team, Email and Phone cells. Six icons
  on every row cost more width than the values beside them, and you read
  past all of them to reach the data. The **Actions** column — now
  labelled — carries a single copy button next to the edit pencil. It
  opens a short list of that player's details; press **Copy** beside the
  one you want and the window closes with it on the clipboard.

- **Phone numbers are readable.** `(206) 555-1234` rather than the run of
  digits a registration form collected. Only numbers FreePair can
  recognise for certain are regrouped — an international number is shown
  exactly as entered, because guessing at the grouping of a number whose
  country is unknown produces something that looks authoritative and is
  wrong. Nothing is rewritten in your event file either way.

- **Columns are tighter**, now that they are not making room for icons
  that have gone.

### Sections list

- **The event's totals sit under the Sections heading** — *4 sections ·
  120 players* — so the size of an event is a glance rather than a column
  of numbers added up by hand. Deleted sections count towards neither.

### Smaller things

- **The Pairing Operations menu prints its keyboard shortcuts**, `F5`
  beside **Pair Next Round** and `Ctrl+Shift+Delete` beside **Delete last
  round**. Only those two: the other entries on that menu act on the
  section, while the function keys that look like their match act on the
  whole event, and a menu that names a key which does something else is
  worse than one that names none.

- **The link and lookup buttons in the USCF export dialog no longer
  overlap** the ID box beside them.

## Installer

Windows x64. The installer bundles the .NET runtime, so there is nothing
to install first. Run it and FreePair updates in place, keeping your
settings and recent-events list.

## Known limitations

**Platforms.** This release is Windows only. macOS and Linux are still on
`v0.90.20260829` and will be brought level on the next full release.

**Round robins and quads have no pairing preview.** Their whole schedule
is computed at once and is not a judgement call, so there is nothing to
accept — which also means those pairings reach the Live Score Board as
soon as they are generated. The board shows a round robin's first
unfinished round, so the room is not sent to a board it is not due at.

**Phone formatting is North American.** Seven, ten and eleven digit
numbers are regrouped; anything else is left exactly as it was typed.

**Pairing fidelity.** FreePair's USCF engine is validated against a large
real-world SwissSys corpus and reproduces the great majority of pairings
exactly, reaching 100% on many representative events. The remaining
differences are in areas where the rules leave the director discretion —
board ordering within a score group, and which player takes a bye when
several are equally eligible — so both answers are legal.
