# FreePair v0.67.20260811 (Windows)

A legibility release. Everything here came from directors watching each
other work — the byes you have to verify between rounds, the results you
have to double-check against the pairing sheet, and the two buttons you
reach for most. Nothing about how pairings are computed has changed.

## What's new

**Results now line up in columns.** Directors are trained to enter every
White result off the pairing sheet and then scan straight down the Black
side as a cross-check. That was hard to do when `1-0`, `0-1` and
`1/2-1/2` all started at the same left edge but broke in different
places. Each result is now laid out with White on the left, Black on the
right and the dash between them always in the same spot, so both sides
form a clean column you can run your eye down. The drop-down list matches,
so it reads the same while you are picking.

**Byes are split into their own columns on Standings.** They used to
share one cell — `1/2: 3 4   0: 4` — which meant decoding two kinds of
bye out of one line. Standings now shows **HPB** and **0-PB** side by
side, exactly as the Roster already did, and each sorts on its own.

**Byes are numbered, and counted.** The bye list under the pairings now
reads `Byes this round (3)` with each bye numbered `1.`, `2.`, `3.` — so
checking them against what you wrote down is reading, not counting.

**A round inventory at a glance.** The Board Pairings header now tells you
`12 boards • 24 players playing • 3 paired out`. The board count is what
you actually need to set up, which matters when you are working out how
many tables fit in each room, and the paired-out figure is how many people
to go find if you offer side games.

**Double-click a bye to edit that bye.** Double-clicking a player's HPB or
0-PB cell on the Roster opens their record with **Bye Requests** already
showing, instead of leaving you to find the tab. Double-clicking anywhere
else on the row behaves as before.

**Pair and un-pair a section without leaving the sidebar.** Each section
card now carries a **pair** button and a **delete pairings** button, the
two things you do most between rounds. Both tell you exactly what they
will do before they do it, and deleting still asks you to confirm. If
pairing is not possible yet, the button says why — unentered results, or
no players.

**Sections are reordered by dragging.** The up and down arrows on each
section have gone; use the grip on the left of the section name instead.
It is quicker for moving a section more than one place, and it made room
for the pair and delete buttons.

## Installer

Windows installer, `FreePair-win-Setup.exe`. The .NET runtime and both
pairing engines are bundled — nothing else to install. Existing
installations update in place and keep your settings.

## Known limitations

- **The result columns are sized for the standard grid text.** If you
  scale the pairings text up a long way, a double-forfeit result may sit
  tighter than the rest.
- **Deleting pairings from the sidebar works one section at a time.**
  Clearing every section at once is still done section by section.
- **The bye count covers the round you are looking at**, not the event as
  a whole; switch rounds to see each one.
- **Numbered byes appear on the Pairings tab only.** The Byes tab keeps
  its plain list, since it is already grouped by round.
