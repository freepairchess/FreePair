# FreePair v0.153.20261017

## What's new

- **Separate result-entry PINs for your team.** Enable NA Chess Hub result entry with a default Venue iPad PIN, then add, label, or revoke up to 16 PINs for other helpers. The Results Who column records the label of the PIN used for each saved result; changing a label later does not rewrite earlier attribution. PINs that cannot be recovered after restarting FreePair must be set again or replaced before hosted entry resumes.
- **Hosted entry resumes reliably.** Disabling and re-enabling results entry, including after a restart, keeps the NA Chess Hub configuration and PIN revisions in step. A new PIN does not change an existing helper's assignment.
- **Know which NA Chess Hub account is connected.** The window title names the signed-in account. The toolbar's Log in button becomes NA Chess Hub after sign-in, with My Portal [account name], Log out and Log in as a different user. The portal opens on the NA Chess Hub host selected in Settings, including test environments.
- **Event setup fixes.** ChessAction event import handles the latest registration data, and the merge-sections dialog keeps the director's section selection intact.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **A portal may request a browser sign-in.** FreePair's saved NA Chess Hub sign-in and the browser's session are separate; an unknown account type has no portal shortcut.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
