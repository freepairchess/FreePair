# FreePair v0.146.20261009

## What's new

- **Remove published pairings without touching the event.** From Share → Pairing sharing or the pairing menus, confirm deletion of the pairing/results JSON published on NA Chess Hub. The uploaded tournament file, other files, live scoreboard and local FreePair event remain untouched. A later publish can put pairings back online.
- **Find pairing actions faster.** The event toolbar now groups Pair All Sections and Delete Pairings under **Pairing - All Sections**; section actions are under **Pairing - Current Section**. **Undo** and **Redo** now live in **Edit**, with their keyboard shortcuts unchanged. Event-file commands use the shorter **New**, **Open**, **Save** and **Close** labels.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and engines included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **NA Chess Hub features need a connection and event upload passcode.** Deleting a published pairing does not disable automatic publishing; a later pairing or result update may republish it.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
