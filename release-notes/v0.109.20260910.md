# FreePair v0.109.20260910

## What's new

### Correct pairing points after forfeits

- **Forfeit wins keep their full pairing point.** A result-code translation
  issue could place a player in the wrong score group for the next round.
  Forfeits now retain their points without counting as played opponents or
  colours. This does not force every pairing to match another program.

### Manual full-point byes

- **Assign a full-point bye from the roster.** Edit a player and open
  **Bye Requests** to select **Full-point bye (1)** alongside half-point,
  zero-point and no-bye choices for an upcoming unpaired round.
- **Requests persist without awarding points early.** FreePair saves the
  assignment, excludes the player from that round's games and records one
  point when the round is paired. Automatic odd-player byes continue to
  operate separately on the remaining pairing pool.
- **Byes remain consistent across views.** Requested full-point byes appear
  in the roster, bye displays, wall charts and related PDFs. Unpairing and
  pairing again preserves the manual request without duplicating points.

### More colour choices and recoverable messages

- **Eight additional themes.** Banana Red, Warm Sun Orange, Cornflower Blue,
  Jade Green, Melo Pink, Azure, Lemon Yellow and Royal Purple are available
  under **Settings > Display > Theme**, with adaptive light/dark surfaces
  and readable selected text.
- **Find notices under Event > Messages.** Dismissal offers a short
  **Open Messages** hint; when no active notice remains, there is no empty
  message row taking room from the event workspace.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon and
  macOS Intel packages include the runtime, both pairing engines and guide.
- **Windows signed as Chessnique LLC.** The installer uses the FreePair
  logo and Windows packages are signed and timestamped in the build pipeline.
- **Back up your event before updating.** Use the package for your operating
  system and processor and keep your normal event backups.

## Known limitations

- **Future manual full-point requests are FreePair-specific.** They are
  saved in a dedicated SJSON field that SwissSys may not honor. Completed
  byes remain normal round results. Unsupported standalone pairing-TRF
  exports reject these requests explicitly rather than silently mispairing.
- **Some SwissSys differences remain.** Opponent, bye and colour choices
  can still differ. Further colour/floater investigation is paused; agreement
  with another program is not a rules verdict.
- **macOS Developer ID signing and notarization are not configured.**
  Gatekeeper may block those downloads under local security policy.
- **Linux publisher signatures are not added.** Windows signing does not
  change native macOS or Linux trust behavior.
- **Windows security prompts may still appear.** Signing does not bypass
  SmartScreen reputation, antivirus policies or administrator permissions.
- **Message history is session-only.** Closing the window clears it.
