# FreePair v0.160.20261110

## What's new

### NA Chess Hub account and pairing access

- **See your options before signing in.** Account status is now available from the Log in menu. It explains free local pairing for up to 20 players and 4 rounds, free pairing of eligible NA Chess Hub events, and how to purchase a FreePair membership for unlimited pairing.
- **Clearer access for organizers, delegates and players.** Account status shows what an organizer can pair, what their event delegates can pair, and the free local limit. Players see their membership status, expiry and pairing options in separate, easy-to-read lines, with a direct membership link. Verified offline-access expiry appears when available.
- **Free Hub pairing follows the event's director role and roster.** Organizer-owned and delegated events qualify when their roster is hosted on NA Chess Hub. Being registered as a player, knowing an event passcode, or using an external roster does not unlock free Hub pairing. FreePair checks access online.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and pairing engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and chess pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **Membership access needs a verified signed proof.** Signing in or purchasing membership alone does not unlock paid pairing until NA Chess Hub issues a valid signed entitlement. Offline access ends when the proof or membership expires; event downloads and Hub roster checks still need internet.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
