# FreePair v0.154.20261019

## What's new

- **Free local tournaments.** Run events with up to 20 players and four rounds without signing in. Add players manually or import a CSV roster.
- **Your NA Chess Hub events remain free.** After signing in, organizers and delegated tournament directors can download and pair events they are authorized to manage through My Events. NA Chess Hub checks each download; registration as a player alone does not grant organizer access.
- **Membership for other events.** Pair larger local events or use other event sources with an active NA Chess Hub membership and verified signed access. The Help → Using FreePair table explains the options and links directly to membership purchase.
- **Check account and membership status.** NA Chess Hub → Account status shows the connected account, membership details reported by NA Chess Hub, and whether FreePair has verified signed access. Refresh entitlement after purchasing or renewing; a reported membership alone cannot unlock paid features.
- **Organizer logos stay current.** Clicking an event organizer's logo refreshes its cached image and opens the event on NA Chess Hub.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and pairing engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **Signed membership needs periodic renewal.** FreePair must receive a verified entitlement from NA Chess Hub. If the signing service is temporarily unavailable, previously verified access lasts only until its signed expiration, at most five minutes from issuance. Free local tournaments and authorized own/delegated event downloads do not depend on membership.
- **Linux account sign-in needs a credential keyring.** Install and unlock a Secret Service-compatible keyring to store NA Chess Hub credentials securely. Free local use does not require sign-in.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
