# FreePair v0.53

A **result-entry refresh** fix. When you enter or correct a game result,
FreePair now updates the **Standings**, **Wall Chart**, and the Roster tab's
**Score** column immediately — including when you fix a result in a round that
was already complete. This was most visible in double-round events (where each
pairing is played twice, "Game 1 of 2" and "Game 2 of 2"), but it applies to any
event where you go back and correct a finished round. Upgrading is low-risk and
recommended.

## What's new in v0.53

### Live standings after every result

- **Standings and Wall Chart update the moment you enter a result.** Previously,
  editing a result inside a round that was already fully scored could leave the
  Standings and Wall Chart showing the old numbers until something else forced a
  refresh. They now recompute on every result change.
- **Double-round events fixed.** In a Double Swiss (or other two-game) event,
  correcting a "Game 2 of 2" result now flows straight through to the standings,
  team standings, and wall chart.
- **Roster Score column stays in sync.** The **Score** column on the Roster tab
  now tracks each player's running score as results come in, instead of staying
  at `0.0`.

## Known limitations

- **Re-publish to refresh online results.** NA Chess Hub keeps a previously
  uploaded results file until an event is re-published. After correcting a result,
  publish the event again so the updated standings reach the online results page.
