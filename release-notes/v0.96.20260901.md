# FreePair v0.96.20260901

## What's new

**One coin toss for the whole event.** US Chess rule 28J has the TD flip
a coin to decide who plays White on board 1, and every section then
follows that one toss. FreePair used to toss a fresh coin for each
section, so a five-section event was five independent tosses and its top
boards could contradict one another. The coin is now flipped once, the
first time a round 1 needs it, and every other section reads it back.

**A new Coin Toss tab on the event page.** It shows what the coin gave
and when it was tossed — the line to read out if a board-1 colour is ever
questioned — and lets you make the toss ahead of time, before pairing
anything. The toss is saved inside the event file, so it survives closing
and reopening across a multi-day event. It freezes as soon as any section
pairs a round, because changing it then would leave the sections already
paired on one toss and the rest on another.

Choosing "Top seed plays White" for a single section is still available
and is still a decision about that section alone. Events paired before
this release are untouched; their colours stay exactly as they were.

**Delete Pairings now lets you choose what goes.** The event-wide delete
used to be all-or-nothing: every pairing and every result, or nothing.
The usual reason to reach for it is much smaller — a round paired too
early, or the wrong section paired — and the only tool on offer charged
you the whole event to fix it.

The new dialog lists every section with its rounds, and says of each
round whether all results are in, some are, or it was paired and never
scored. **It opens with only the unscored rounds ticked**, so pressing
delete straight away undoes a mispairing and touches nothing that was
played. You can extend the selection as far as the whole event, and a
warning appears the moment your choice would discard results you typed
in. Confirming shows a summary of exactly what will go, section by
section, before anything is removed. **Ctrl+Shift+K** opens it.

Rounds are deleted from the last one backwards, because each player's
history is kept in order — so ticking a round also ticks the rounds after
it in that section, and the dialog says so.

**The keyboard shortcut list moved to Help.** It was the last tab of App
Settings and the only thing there that changes nothing. Looking up a
keystroke is a question, so it now lives under **Help → Keyboard
Shortcuts**, which is also where somebody who does not already know where
it is will look.

**"My events on NA Chess Hub" now names the account.** The dialog offered
sign-in when you were signed out and then said nothing once you were in.
Since NA Chess Hub can sign you in from your browser's existing session
without showing a page, it was possible to be looking at a short list of
somebody else's events and read it as your own going missing.

## Installer

Windows x64. The installer bundles the .NET runtime, the pairing engines
and the user guide — nothing else to install. Run it and FreePair
updates itself in place; your events and settings are untouched.

## Known limitations

- **Windows only in this release.** macOS and Linux stay on the previous
  version and will catch up on the next full run.
- **The coin toss is not applied retrospectively.** Events already paired
  carry no recorded toss, and nothing about their existing colours
  changes.
- **A round cannot be deleted from the middle.** Player history is kept
  in order, so clearing round 2 necessarily clears round 3 as well. This
  is a deliberate trade-off: the alternative silently renumbers games
  that were played.
- **Keyboard shortcuts cannot be reassigned.**
