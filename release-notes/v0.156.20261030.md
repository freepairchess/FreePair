# FreePair v0.156.20261030

## What's new

- **Result colors now match NA Chess Hub.** Forfeits, including double forfeits, appear purple; a lower-rated player's win appears red; and a draw between players whose displayed ratings differ by at least 200 points appears yellow. Unplayed boards and byes are not highlighted. Forfeits take priority over rating-based colors, so a forfeit is never mistaken for a played upset.
- **Clearer meaning for unrated results.** A displayed rating of 0 participates in the result-color comparison, just as on NA Chess Hub. A red highlight is a visual cue, not an upset-prize decision: unrated wins and forfeits still do not qualify for Biggest Upset.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and pairing engine included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **Colors use the displayed ratings.** They describe the recorded result; prize eligibility has separate rules, so an unrated player's win can be shown in red without qualifying for Biggest Upset.
- **Linux account sign-in needs a credential keyring.** Install and unlock a Secret Service-compatible keyring to store NA Chess Hub credentials securely.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
