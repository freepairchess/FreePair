# FreePair v0.111.20260912

## What's new

### Faster section cleanup

- **Delete many sections at once.** **Event Operations > Delete Sections**
  lets you remove a batch of sections without opening each one, especially
  useful after splitting a large roster into many quads.
- **Choose the scope before deleting.** Start with nothing selected, use
  **Select All Unpaired Sections**, **Deselect All**, or the red **Select All**
  button. Unpaired means the section has no existing rounds.
- **A clear warning protects your event.** Confirmation names the selected
  sections and warns that their entire rosters, pairings and results will
  be removed. Cancel changes nothing; Undo can restore the batch.

### Easier player lookup

- **Search where you add the player.** **Roster Update > Add** opens the
  player form directly. Its **Search Player DB** button starts with the name
  you typed, and your chosen match fills the same form for review.
- **You stay in control of the roster.** Searching does not add anyone until
  you press **Add**. Cancelling search keeps your manual entries, and
  unrelated details and bye choices stay intact when you choose a player.
- **Older lookups cannot overwrite your choice.** Changing an ID or opening
  player search prevents a late verification response from replacing the
  player you are currently entering.

### Accurate published forfeits

- **Forfeits stay marked as forfeits on NA Chess Hub.** Published board
  results now retain forfeit-win, forfeit-loss and double-forfeit markers,
  and player histories use the corresponding `X` and `F` codes rather than
  ordinary wins and losses. Individual and team-board results are covered.
- **A double forfeit is visibly different from a played game.** Both players
  still receive zero points, but the result is shown as `(0F-0F)`, not an
  unexplained `(0-0)`. Scores and opponent references are unchanged.

## Installer

- **Windows x64 only.** Run `FreePair-win-x64-Setup.exe`. The installer
  includes the .NET runtime, both pairing engines and the updated user guide.
  Keep your normal event backups before updating.
- **Signed as Chessnique LLC.** The local Windows release process signs and
  timestamps the installer and packaged executables before publication.
- **macOS and Linux remain on v0.109.20260910.** This release does not replace
  their existing packages.

## Known limitations

- **Previously published results need republishing.** Install this update and
  publish the event again to replace an older results JSON that omitted
  forfeit markers. The saved `.sjson` already retains that information.
- **Previously exported reports are snapshots.** Regenerate reports after
  deleting sections. The normal Restore action is not permanent-deletion
  recovery; use Undo while the change is available or your event backup.
- **Existing pairing differences remain.** This release does not change the
  pairing algorithms or their deliberate differences from SwissSys.
- **Windows security prompts can still appear.** Signing does not override
  SmartScreen, antivirus policy or administrator permissions.
