# FreePair v0.144.20261009

## What's new

- **Share a live scoreboard with more control.** New events start with eight seconds on each screen. The timing and optional earlier rounds are saved in the event file; turning on earlier rounds sends their results to NA Chess Hub without restarting sharing.
- **Manage the public scoreboard from Share.** The Share window keeps its scoreboard controls together, lets you edit the event ID and upload passcode, and can remove the public scoreboard from NA Chess Hub after confirmation when sharing is stopped. If another FreePair instance owns the event, sharing now reports that conflict instead of retrying indefinitely.
- **Find and share pairing pages sooner.** Pairing Sharing shows the clickable NA Chess Hub pairing address before upload, with copy and browser actions beside Publish now. The public QR and link appear after a successful upload.
- **Print the pairings you need on fewer sheets.** Pairings in 1 page lets you choose paired rounds and print an alphabetical view, a by-section view, or both. All rounds of a quad can share a single sheet, with extra room to write the first round's start time.

## Installer

- **All four platforms.** Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple silicon and macOS Intel builds are included.
- **Runtime and engines included.** Installers and portable builds bundle the required .NET runtime and pairing engine.

## Known limitations

- **NA Chess Hub features require an internet connection and a compatible service.** Removing a public scoreboard does not delete the local event or its credentials.
- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When a source event does not record why a legal board-order or bye choice was made, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
