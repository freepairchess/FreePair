# FreePair v0.68.20260812

Directors can now see when a NA Chess Hub sync is worth doing, clear a
whole event's pairings in one step, and — on Mac in particular — reach
parts of the app that were previously off-screen or impossible to grab.
One correctness fix is included: see **Byes for a round you unpaired**
below.

## What's new

**FreePair tells you when a sync would be useful.** No more re-syncing
just to find out whether anything has changed. While an event is open,
FreePair quietly checks NA Chess Hub and shows a red count on each
section — new registrations, bye requests added or cancelled, check-ins —
with a running total beside **Sections** so it is visible whichever
section you are on. Opening an event checks straight away, which answers
"did anything happen overnight?" without asking.

**Click the count to deal with it.** The number on a section opens the
review for *that* section; the total opens the review for the whole
event. Nothing is ever applied automatically — you still choose what to
take and when, so you can leave three or four changes to accumulate
before re-pairing, or look at projected pairings first.

You can turn the checking off, or change how often it runs, under
**Settings → NA Chess Hub**. It checks every 30 seconds by default.

**Delete every pairing in an event in one step.** New under **Event
Operations**, directly below Pair All Sections. It clears every round of
every section so you can pair the event again from round 1; players,
their seeding and their requested byes are all kept.

Because this is the most destructive thing FreePair can do, it asks
first, in a way designed to be hard to do by accident: a red warning
naming every section and round it would clear and how many recorded
results would be discarded, with **Cancel as the default button** — so
Enter and Esc both back out, and only clicking the labelled button
proceeds. A copy of your event is saved beforehand, so you can still go
back through **Save Event → Earlier versions**.

**Byes for a round you unpaired.** If you paired a round, published it,
then unpaired it, adding a player afterwards could offer to give them a
result for that round — and could quietly record a zero-point bye for a
round the section was no longer paired for. It did not show on the Byes
tab, but it counted in the standings and the wall chart. FreePair now
looks only at the rounds that actually exist on your computer, whatever
the website still shows. The same fix covers adding a player by hand and
editing one.

**Scrollbars you can actually grab.** The horizontal scrollbar under a
wide grid was a hairline that would not respond to the mouse — the
vertical one worked, which made it look as though sideways scrolling was
broken. Both are now full size and always present.

**Dialogs stay on the screen.** A dialog taller than your display could
be cut off at the bottom, taking its buttons with it, with no way to
resize or scroll to them. Dialogs are now kept within the screen and can
be resized if they need the room. This mostly affected Mac laptops, and
anyone who had increased the app text size.

## Installer

Windows, macOS and Linux builds. The .NET runtime and both pairing
engines are bundled — nothing else to install. Existing installations
update in place and keep your settings.

## Known limitations

- **Sync checking needs an event ID and passcode.** Events without NA
  Chess Hub credentials show no counts, which is expected.
- **The count is what a sync would bring in, not a history.** It reflects
  the difference right now; it does not list what changed since you last
  looked.
- **Checking is per open event.** Closing the event stops it.
- **Deleting all pairings works on the event you have open**, and skips
  any section you have soft-deleted.
- **Going back does not un-publish or un-print.** Pairings already handed
  out or already sent to NA Chess Hub stay as they are until you publish
  again.
- **Scrollbars now always take up their full width.** On a very small
  screen that is a little more chrome than before, in exchange for being
  usable.
