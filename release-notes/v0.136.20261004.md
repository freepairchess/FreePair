# FreePair v0.136.20261004

## What's new

- **Hosted result entry now shows the right title and pairing rating.** NA Chess Hub's result-entry page receives each player's chess title and the same pairing rating FreePair publishes for the event, including custom and regional ratings. Players with the same name remain distinct; an unknown rating stays blank, while an explicitly unrated player stays unrated.
- **Roster updates refresh hosted entry.** Editing a title or pairing rating sends a new snapshot without changing players' or games' identities. Previously hosted events refresh their player details when sharing reconnects.

## Installer

- **Windows x64 release.** macOS and Linux remain on the previous all-platform release.
- **Runtime and engines included.** The installer bundles the required .NET runtime and pairing engines.

## Known limitations

- **Some discretionary choices can differ from SwissSys.** When the source event does not record a director's rationale for a legal board-order or bye choice, FreePair may make a different permitted choice.
